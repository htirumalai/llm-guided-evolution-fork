Here are several ways to ask about improving code performance by adjusting default parameters or hyperparameters, ranging from brief to detailed, and emphasizing different aspects of the question:

**Short & Sweet:**

* How can we tune the parameters or hyperparameters to improve this code's performance?
* What parameter/hyperparameter changes could improve this code?
* What parameter/hyperparameter tweaks could optimize this code?

**More In-Depth:**

* What specific parameter or hyperparameter changes are most likely to enhance the performance of this code?
* What parameter and/or hyperparameter adjustments would be most effective in optimizing this code's performance (e.g., speed, accuracy, resource usage)?
* To improve this code's performance, which parameters or hyperparameters should we adjust from their defaults, and what is the rationale behind those adjustments?

**Focusing on Specific Improvements:**

* How can we modify the parameters or hyperparameters to specifically improve the [speed/accuracy/efficiency/etc.] of this code?

The most appropriate wording will depend on the context and your desired level of detail.
